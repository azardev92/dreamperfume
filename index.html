A visit

How a visit goes.

Come as you are. Tell us what you usually wear, or that you are starting from nothing. We will pull a short edit.

  1. 01

    On your skin

    Paper strips only start the conversation. A scent counts once it has sat on you for a while.

  2. 02

    Unhurried

    There is no cart on this website, and no need to decide in the first minute you are in the shop.

  3. 03

    Leave with it

    A bottle, or a gift finished with ribbon while you wait. Or simply a name to remember next time.

The house

A small tray, chosen slowly.

We would rather you try six scents properly than leave with a bottle that tires you by evening. What you see online is a sample of the house. The tray in the boutique is where it becomes personal.

  • 01
    Smell it here

    The website can introduce a name. It cannot tell you how that name sits on your skin.

  • 02
    Ask for a direction

    Fresh for the day, something warmer after dark, or a floral that stays soft. We will narrow the tray.

  • 03
    Hours and the door

    The address, the phone, and when we are open are kept on the contact page.
